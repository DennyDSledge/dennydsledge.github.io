Denny D'Sledge is a singer-songwriter bringing big Texas tone and honest songs to the stage. This page is an introduction: who he is, what he sounds like, and where he's headed. The details are best shared in conversation.

Blues-rock in the tradition of Stevie Ray Vaughan: guitar out front, a voice with some grit in it, and songs that tell a story without getting in their own way. Music made for the room. Warm, loud, a little rowdy, and always sincere.

Released via DistroKid and streaming on the major platforms. Start with the album Demo Gears for the whole picture, then hear featured songs like "Vengeance" and "Sophie Cunningham (Mine)". Links are below.

Denny is seriously considering making Austin home. It's where Stevie Ray Vaughan made his name, a city that shows up for live music night after night, and an entertainment community where music and comedy meet.

Live music, podcasts and entertainment work are all on the table, from club nights to creative collaborations. Beyond the music, Denny is developing original concepts as an intellectual property entrepreneur. Specifics are shared privately with serious parties.

The full catalog is streaming everywhere listed below. If something here sparks interest, reach out directly by phone or email. Thanks for listening.
