How it works

From inspection report to clear to close.

  1. Send the inspection report or BINSR

    A realtor, buyer or seller refers the deal, with the BINSR deadline and closing date.

  2. We triage the deal

    Closing date, title company, occupancy, year built (pre-1978 homes trigger lead-safe rules) and the type of work involved.

  3. Written bid from a licensed contractor

    Our licensed Arizona contractor partner walks the property and issues a written bid within [X] business days.

  4. BINSR response and signed agreement

    The repairs are offered through the program. The seller signs the written repair agreement and an escrow payment authorization.

  5. About 25% toward materials

    Materials are ordered and the work is scheduled ahead of the closing date.

  6. Work completed and documented

    Photos, permits where required, and independent clearance testing for mold where the scope calls for it.

  7. Balance paid at closing

    The final invoice goes to escrow, and the balance is paid from the sale proceeds at closing.
